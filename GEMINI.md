# Parenting App — Project Rules

This workspace is dedicated to building the **Parenting App** — a calm, one-handed daily co-pilot for new parents. It is intentionally isolated from `Personal-OS` and other workspaces.

## 1. Product & Content Principles
Synthesize practical daily guidance from three core frameworks plus evidence-based baby/parent care:
- ***Hunter, Gather, Parent* (TEAM):** Togetherness (include baby in real life rather than constant entertainment), calm co-regulation, early autonomy, minimal interference/over-praising.
- ***Bringing Up Bébé* (*Le Cadre*):** *La Pause* (briefly pausing before intervening at night), predictable daily rhythm over rigid scheduling, calm boundaries, and varied real-food exposure.
- ***The Book You Wish Your Parents Had Read*:** Rupture & repair over perfection, validating all feelings while holding boundaries, noticing parent triggers, and treating behavior as communication.

## 2. UX & Tone Guardrails
- **One-handed & Night-first:** Large bottom-reach tap targets, minimal typing, warm/low-blue dark mode for 3 AM use.
- **Anti-guilt, anti-anxiety:** No red warning badges, no broken-streak shaming, no comparison feeds.
- **Stage-aware & Just-in-Time:** Surface only what matters for the baby's current age/week and deliver short, contextual wisdom in the moment.
- **Safety:** Always include a calm "SOS / Overwhelmed" flow with safe-sleep reminders (e.g., permission to place baby safely in crib for 2 minutes if caregiver is at their limit). Never present medical diagnoses—encourage consulting a pediatrician for medical concerns.

## 3. Engineering Standards
- Keep architecture modular and simple; confirm tech stack and MVP scope before scaffolding dependencies.
- Write clean, well-tested code with clear separation between UI, state/sync, and stage-aware content data.
